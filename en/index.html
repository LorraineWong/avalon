HOW TO PLAY

The four parts of every game

Good needs three successful Quests and must then survive the Assassin. Evil wins with three failed Quests or five rejected proposals in a row.

1 · Build a team

The Leader picks the required number of players and may include themselves. Leadership rotates.

2 · Vote together

The team needs more than half the table to approve it. Every vote is revealed together.

3 · Take the Quest

Good can only play Success. Evil may play Fail. Only the total number of Fail cards is revealed.

4 · Decide the winner

After three successes, the Assassin can still identify Merlin and steal the game for Evil.

The fourth Quest

With seven or more players, Quest four needs two Fail cards to fail.

Five rejections

If five teams for the same Quest are rejected in a row, Evil wins immediately.

ROLE GUIDE

All 17 characters

Start with the classic lineup. Explore both alignments below; advanced characters appear only when enabled by the host.

Good characters
Good

Merlin

You know the evil players (except Mordred). Stay hidden — if the Assassin finds you, Evil wins.

Good

Percival

You see Merlin and Morgana, but cannot tell which is which. Protect the real Merlin.

Good

Loyal Servant

No special knowledge. Use discussion and votes to find the traitors.

Good

Good Lancelot

You are Good. In the base Lancelot rules, you and Evil Lancelot know each other.

Good

Cleric

At the start of the game, you learn whether the initial Leader is Good or Evil.

Good

Troublemaker

You are Good but must show Evil whenever your loyalty is checked.

Good

Untrustworthy Servant

You are Good and know the Assassin, but Merlin sees you as Evil. After three successful Quests, the Assassin first tries to recruit you.

Evil characters
Evil

Assassin

If Good completes 3 quests, you pick one player to assassinate. Hit Merlin and Evil wins.

Evil

Morgana

You appear as Merlin to Percival.

Evil

Mordred

Merlin cannot see you as evil.

Evil

Oberon

You do not know the other evil players and they do not know you (Merlin does see you).

Evil

Minion of Mordred

You know your evil allies (except Oberon). Sabotage the quests.

Evil

Evil Lancelot

You are Evil. In the base Lancelot rules, you and Good Lancelot know each other.

Evil

Lunatic

You are Evil and must play Fail on every Quest you join.

Evil

Brute

You are Evil but may play Fail only on the first three Quests.

Evil

Revealer

You are Evil and must expose your identity after the second failed Quest.

Evil

Trickster

You are Evil and may show either loyalty whenever your loyalty is checked.

Optional modules

Lady of the Lake (optional, 7+): after Quests 2, 3 and 4 the holder secretly checks one player's loyalty, then passes the Lady to them. Former holders cannot be checked.

Excalibur lets its holder replace another team member’s Quest card. The Lancelot Allegiance variant draws an allegiance card at the start of each Quest; in this mode the two Lancelots do not know each other.

FAQ

Common questions

Do we need an app or an account?

No. Everyone opens the room link in a current browser and enters a game name.

Do we have to be in the same place?

No. Talk around a real table, or keep a voice or video call open for remote play.

Can another player read my role?

Roles and actions are encrypted in the browser. This protects against normal clients and casual database inspection, but not a compromised host browser or a deliberately modified client.

Is this an official product?

No. It is a free, unofficial fan project with no affiliation, sponsorship, or endorsement from the original game's designers, publishers, or rights holders.
