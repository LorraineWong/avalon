## Data and terms

Support opens Ko-fi in a new tab. Ko-fi, processors and the creator may receive
payment/contact data under their policies; the game does not collect card
details or store donor data in rooms. The shared privacy additions disclose this
in seven languages. Existing blanket no-refund copy was replaced with reference
to the actual provider's payment/refund terms and applicable law.

The site rendering/approved URLs live in `site/assets/js/project.js`, translated
copy in `site/language/project-copy.js`, and the static English fallback in
`site/pages/support/index.html`. Keep them aligned. Account passwords, bank
information, verification documents and payment API secrets never belong in
this repository.

Do not promise anonymity on payment receipts: personal PayPal commonly displays
legal name/email, while Stripe has public business/support details and a
statement descriptor. The owner should review their actual receipt settings.
Provider terms, identity checks and any real test payment remain owner actions.
