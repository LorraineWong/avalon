About & support

A personal project by Lorraine Wong.

Made for our game nights

I wanted to play Avalon at gatherings with colleagues and friends, but we did not always have the cards with us. The online versions I tried had interfaces I found awkward, required an account, or ran into bugs during play. I decided to try building my own with AI: a simple way to open a room and play together without registering a player account.

Help keep good game nights going

If this project has made your game night easier, you are welcome to support its upkeep. Voluntary support can help with domain renewals, development, bug fixes and any future necessary service costs. Hosting and Firebase currently use free plans.

Playing is free. Supporting the project gives no extra roles, features or advantages. Please also support the official board game and its creators.
