Play Avalon Online Free

PlayAvalonNow is a free, unofficial online adaptation of Avalon, the social deduction board game for 5–10 players. Play in a private browser room on your phone or desktop, with no signup or download.

How to play with friends

Choose a nickname, create a room and share its link or code with your friends. Each player uses their own device. Discuss in person or over a voice call, propose a team, vote and complete quests. Hidden roles include Merlin, Percival, Morgana and the Assassin.

Good wins by completing three quests and protecting Merlin from the Assassin. Evil wins by failing three quests, rejecting five teams in a row or correctly assassinating Merlin after three successful quests.
